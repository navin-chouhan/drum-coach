Task: the student just reached a new stage on their drum journey and earned a **show-off song**: a real, well-known
song whose drum part they can now play for friends. The input has "stage" (the stage they passed), "canDo" (what
they can now play) and "goals" (styles they like; may be empty).

Pick 3 songs whose main drum part uses only what's in "canDo" (or simpler), at a tempo a beginner can hold, ideally
in the styles in "goals". Prefer famous songs friends will recognise, with a steady, repeating groove and few fills.
Don't pick songs whose drum part is far beyond the stage even if the song is easy to sing.

"why" is one encouraging sentence for the student: what in the drum part matches what they just learned.

Answer shape: { "songs": [ { "title": string, "artist": string, "why": string } ] } (exactly 3)
