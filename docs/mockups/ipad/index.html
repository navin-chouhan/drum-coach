Drum Coach on iPad · mockups

Fresh iPad-native designs for v1 (decision D4): iPad only, landscape, 1194 × 834 pt, light theme. The example data is a plausible student three weeks in (Stage 3, The money beat); the iPad starts fresh (D3). Each picture links to its page. Notes, changes from the web design and the open questions are in NOTES.md. Re-render with node docs/mockups/ipad/render.cjs.

Home and the lesson player

JourneyCoach line, the next lesson with its moves and kit gestures, rank and numbers, today’s set, the stage song and the trail.
Lesson · before the takeFull screen. Steps, tempo ladder, the coach line, four moves with gestures, the highway preview, the goal.
Lesson · count-inOne bar counted out loud, the big number on the beat, the first notes waiting at the line.
Lesson · during the takeOnly the music, one live tip and Stop (or crash × 4). Hits land on the notes and light the kit.
Lesson · resultsEvery note says on it, early, late or missed; the timing line; one thing to fix; the next move.
Share takeThe take as a MIDI file through the iOS share sheet (D5).
