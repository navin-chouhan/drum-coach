    Hand positioning that a side view can't show: where the stick sits in the hand.

    Why it matters

    The stick pivots between the thumb and index finger; everything else just steers. Squeeze with the back fingers and the stick can't bounce (that's the ✗ Wrong of "Full stroke"). The thumb lies along the side of the stick, not on top.
