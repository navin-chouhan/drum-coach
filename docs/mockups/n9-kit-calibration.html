A · Calibration, new step: "soft, then loud" (after "hit each pad once")

Each pad answers differently: one gives 40 for a soft stroke, another 15. This step measures each pad's soft and loud, so accents, ghost notes and "bring it up" are judged the same on every pad. The loud strokes also measure the pad's own retriggers (the double-notes some pads send), so the app filters exactly those. About 2 minutes for the whole kit.

B · Sound check from the throne

Today the sound check is three sliders. From the kit you can't reach the mouse, so the app plays each sound and you answer with a pad; it adjusts the level and plays it again until you say OK.

C · Left-handed

One switch in Setup → Pads ("I play left-handed: hi-hat on my right, lead with my left"). Every hint, intro slide and "How it moves" mirrors: R ↔ L. Scoring is the same (the kit tells the app which pad, not which hand).
