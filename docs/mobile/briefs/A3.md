# Brief: A3, MIDI module, production (Phase 1)

## Goal
Turn the Gate 0 probe module into the app's production MIDI input. It runs at `apps/mobile/modules/drum-midi`,
implements the `MidiPort` contract, and feeds `@drum-coach/core`'s hit path. It must be hardened against what the real
kit and iPad showed.

## Read first
`CLAUDE.md` (hard facts), `docs/mobile/GATE0.md` (the whole file, especially §3 design, §5.1 and **§9 device
results**), `spikes/midi-probe/modules/drum-midi/**` (your starting point: compiled and verified on the iPad),
`spikes/midi-probe/src/shared/*` and `App.tsx` (how the TS side used it), `apps/mobile/src/platform/contracts.ts`
(`MidiPort`, `NativeMidiPacket`, `MidiInputInfo`; read-only), `docs/mobile/BOARD.md` (B24, B25).

## Verified on the device (don't break)
- `e-Drum` (manufacturer `Medeli`) enumerates over USB-C. Every note, choke, note-off and clock message arrives
  correctly.
- Hot-plug works in under 2 s.
- Host timestamps are excellent: native − host median 0.05 ms, and no `ts=0` packets.
- `MIDIInputPortCreateWithProtocol(._1_0)` with the UMP→bytes unpack works.
- The `DispatchQueue.main.sync` client creation did not hang.

## Fix these (from the device run)
- **B24, bridge delay:** p95 was 40–59 ms in a debug build, because every packet (about 41 clock messages per second)
  was its own JS event and re-render. **Batch natively:** accumulate packets and emit one `onPackets` event with an
  array at most every ~8 ms, or immediately when a batch holds a note-on. Clock-only batches can wait up to ~33 ms.
  Hits must never wait for a clock batch. Clock and active sensing still reach JS: counting and filtering stay in TS
  (CLAUDE.md rule).
- **B25, stale messages on resume:** packets queued while backgrounded arrived on resume with old timestamps (up to
  48 s). In TS (`src/platform/midi.ts`), drop packets whose `hostTimeMs` is more than 250 ms older than `clock.now()`
  at JS receipt when they arrive within 1 s after a foreground event. Count them in `debugInfo`.
- Selection by name: `setPreferredNames(['e-Drum'])` auto-selects on appear and re-selects after replug. Ids can
  change across replug; names are the stable key.

## Owns
- `apps/mobile/modules/drum-midi/**`: the native module (Swift, podspec, `expo-module.config.json`, its TS wrapper
  and types).
- `apps/mobile/src/platform/midi.ts`: `createMidiPort(): MidiPort` and `connectMidiToHitBus(port, bus)`. The latter
  does framing (core `midi/framing`) → for each message, `bus.receiveMidi(bytes, hostTimeMs)` → stale drop. It returns
  an unsubscribe function and live counters.
- `apps/mobile/src/dev/**` and `apps/mobile/app/dev/midi.tsx`: the **MIDI debug screen**. It shows devices with
  select, a live hit log (pad name, velocity, time) driven by `bus.onHit`, counters, batch stats and bridge-delay
  stats (port the spike's `Samples`), and "Copy debug info". It's for Phase 1's gate: "the kit lists and logs hits in
  the app".
- Tests: `apps/mobile/src/platform/midi.test.ts` and `apps/mobile/modules/drum-midi/src/*.test.ts`.
- Swift unit tests where practical: timestamp conversion and batching policy as pure Swift functions, in
  `modules/drum-midi/ios/Tests/`. They can't run here; make them compile-ready and say so.
